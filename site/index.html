    How it works

    Each week a logistic regression is retrained on AP voting from 1999 to 2025 and scores every starting-caliber QB, RB, WR and TE on per-game yards, touchdowns, EPA and turnovers, his team's conference standing, and games played. Shares add up to 100% across the whole pool of about 90 players, so they read as each player's slice of the vote if the season ended today.

    Stats from nflverse. Voting history from Pro Football Reference. Not affiliated with the NFL or the Associated Press.
